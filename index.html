Why, and what it is

The idea in one breath, what we agreed in Prague, and where it takes us by 2030.

Inspiration isn’t taught. It is passed on, leader to leader.

Peer-based learning is what makes development last. So rather than adding another programme, experienced leaders learn a topic together, then carry it back to their peers and teams.

In Prague, we agreed that leadership development is key

The fact base and the future solution, unchanged. Leadership Inspiration is designed to answer both.

The fact base we aligned on

  • Strong onboarding, weak in-role development
  • We offer more than is known
  • We work in silos
  • Self-driven learning is limited
  • Leaders want outside perspective
  • Education is only one element
  • Peer-based is what makes it sustainable
  • Coaching is standard elsewhere

The future solution we asked for

  • Leadership is the same, not by function
  • Built around the Hilti Leadership Framework
  • Company needs and individual needs
  • Level the playing field: standardisation
  • Existing delivery modes: Academy, Summits
  • Internal and external resources
  • No new initiatives in our own entities

Where it takes us

2030

Every L3 to L5 leader has met the Hilti Leadership Framework.“Met” means joined a sprint, or a session run by someone who did: a deliberate bar for reach. Depth comes from those who join.

10,000ft

How the system works

Three movements, one framework running through every topic, a quarterly rhythm, and eleven regions delivering together.

Three movements, one flame

Every sprint runs all three, once a quarter.

Light it

Month 1: apply or be nominated

L4 and L5 leaders, 18+ months in role, step forward or are nominated. A digital session opens the framework.

Fuel it

Month 2: the sprint month

Five weekly 60-minute lunch sessions: input, peer exchange, and trying it out in real work between sessions.

Spread it

Month 3 onwards: in the regions

Inspirers pass it on in moments that already exist: Academy, lunch and learns, Waves, team meetings, MRMF.

One framework, a new topic every sprint

The topics change; the framework does not. Content comes from our portfolio where it exists, from external partners where it doesn’t.

The constant

The Hilti Leadership Framework

Three principles, nine practices, 27 behaviours, woven into every sprint.

What changes for leaders when AI changes the work. Needed now, and not yet in our portfolio.

Inside each movement

Who steps forward, what happens in the sprint month, how inspirers pass it on, and how the flame keeps itself alive.

Light it: who steps forward, and how they join

Lighting inspiration is a choice: leaders step forward, or are nominated.

Who

Existing leaders, 18+ months in role

L4 and L5 first, L3 from 2029. Priority to those who did not go through Leading Leaders.

How they join

Apply, or be nominated

For any sprint that interests them. Regional HR and L&D confirm.

What they give

Carry the topic back

Share it in Academy, lunch and learns, Waves, team meetings and MRMF.

What they get

Recognition

A peer network across regions, and visibility with senior leadership once they have shared.

Anyone eligible can step forward; sharing what they learn earns the honour. Opening it to every experienced leader puts Great Employer for Everyone Everywhere into practice.

Fuel it: the sprint month

One topic, five 60-minute lunch sessions, one a week. Nobody loses a week, and each session builds on what people tried in real work. Example: Leading in the age of AI.

Spread it: inspirers in moments that already exist

One kit per topic, for the inspirer who runs the session. Built once by Global L&D, handed over at the end of the sprint month.
